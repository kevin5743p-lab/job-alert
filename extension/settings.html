    How it works

    The short version of what happens after you press a button, and where your data goes.

    Find jobs for me
    Pulls open postings from the job boards and employer feeds, drops anything already seen, and scores what's left against your CV. Runs on your own Groq key.
    Tailor this job
    Rewrites your CV and writes a cover letter for one specific posting. Every claim has to be traceable to your CV text — it can reorder and re-emphasise what's there, and it can't invent. Doing this by hand is optional: it's the same step Apply runs for you when a job hasn't got a tailored CV yet.
    Apply
    The whole application in one button. Writes the tailored CV and cover letter first if the job hasn't got them, renders them as PDFs, opens the posting, fills the form from your saved answers, attaches the documents plus anything from your Documents, and stops for you the moment a question can't be answered from what it knows. Consent boxes are never ticked automatically.
    Paused sites
    When a site starts refusing us, that one domain rests for a day and everything else keeps running. You can lift it early from the applications page.
    Where your data lives
    Your API keys and Adzuna credentials never leave this browser. Your CV, answers, documents and job list sync to your account so they follow you between machines, and row-level security means the server can only ever return your own rows.
